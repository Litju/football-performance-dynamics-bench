## Numerical and validity audit

The [machine-readable findings](../res440/findings.json) and [canonical tables](../res440/tables.md) remain the authority. Values below record what was checked. The contrast and reversal rows are owner-review evidence only.

| Check | Evidence and disposition |

All evaluation windows have two scoreable scenes. Matches carry equal weight after seed averaging. Window and seed counts do not enlarge the independent sample. |
| Worked ADE example | `history_baseline_comparisons` gives learned 1.6680180412 m and baseline 2.2433 m under the common mask and hierarchy. The published values round to 1.6680 m and 2.2433 m. This compares fitted systems with different historical inputs. |
| Four overall C1/C2 differences | C1 is history minus future ball. C2 is future ball minus future ball and opponents. In absolute XY / displacement order, C1 means are 0.2886 / 0.2382 m and C2 means are 0.1147 / 0.1339 m. All 16 match differences are positive. Paper B retains the estimates. |
| Early absolute-XY C2 reversal | At 0.2–1.0 s, absolute-XY ADE is 0.8501 m with future ball and 0.8810 m with the added opponents, an increase of 0.0309 m. The overall C2 mean stays positive under the opposite subtraction convention (control minus added context). Paper B retains this horizon result. |
| Scope change and prior exposure | Original 234-fit v2 design incomplete. All 216 training LOMO fits deferred and unexecuted.
