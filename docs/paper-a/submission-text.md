# Paper A submission text

## Cover letter

Dear Editors,

Please consider *Football Performance Dynamics Bench: Information-Boundary-Aware Evaluation of Multi-Agent Football Motion* for Transactions on Machine Learning Research.

The manuscript reconstructs the benchmark and result identities of successive football motion studies, then specifies how the information permitted at a prediction origin, the target representation and the evaluated population determine what a trajectory score measures. The reconstruction retains invalidated, negative and unrecoverable outcomes and distinguishes public contract execution from verification of archived evidence.

A four-match descriptive demonstration shows how the evaluation contracts bind frozen checkpoints to common masks, physical metrics and match-level aggregation. The report discloses the incomplete fit design, the research-only qualification state and the limits on releasing tracking data and learned replay artifacts. A related empirical paper is planned around the full comparative analysis. This submission reserves that analysis for the related work and reports one worked example of the benchmark methodology.

Thank you for considering the manuscript.

## Contribution statement

The work contributes a benchmark reconstruction with explicit scientific identities and validity states, an information-boundary-aware evaluation specification, and an evidence chain that distinguishes executable public checks from owner-private replay. The four-match example demonstrates the resulting evaluation workflow. The frozen DeepSets model is a reference system.
