The sections below describe existing methodological contracts, reconstructed historical evidence and a prospective study design. ## 1. Research objects and benchmark identity

FPD Bench distinguishes a research family from a concrete research object and from an executable benchmark. A family records a scientific scope even when no historical task can be recovered. A research object can retain a historical study, an invalidated formulation, a scoped negative result or a candidate formulation. Execution status and scientific maturity are separate attributes: recovery of an evaluator does not restore the validity of the formulation it once scored. The typed descriptors record the question, target, modalities, conditioning information, temporal interpretation, reference frame, population semantics, evaluation unit, causal status and known non-claims. **Evidence:** C1/C6; [typed contracts](../src/fpdbench/benchmarks/base.py), [registry](../src/fpdbench/benchmarks/registry.py), [transition record](benchmark-transitions.md).

| Family | Reconstructed object and admissible interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| `workload_performance_state` | A partial, non-executable whole-session study and a separate invalidated synthetic signed-acceleration formulation. Diagnostic models for the latter are not evidence of whole-session experts. |
| `multimodal_state_estimation` | A synthetic sensor-state pilot with a scoped negative target-selection outcome; no selected benchmark target family. |
| `future_response_forecasting` | Research intent without a recovered historical benchmark, scorer, target, model or horizon assignment. |
| `conditional_multi_agent_motion_prediction` | Recovered absolute-position and origin-relative displacement benchmark identities, with explicit limits on scorer/calibration and model parity. |

These classifications preserve what survived reconstruction rather than imposing a single successful-benchmark narrative. Their authority is the [transition graph](../src/fpdbench/benchmarks/transition_graph.py), the research-object modules and A1/A6's exact registry records. An unrecoverable study is an evidence gap; an invalidated formulation and a scoped negative empirical outcome are different claims.

A benchmark identifies the scientific task. Its dataset membership, measurement state, split protocol, evaluator, checkpoint, experiment, result and public release have separate identities. Population semantics specify the intended subjects and observations; membership specifies the observations actually used. Equal benchmark identifiers therefore do not suffice to establish result comparability. Under the implemented compatibility rules, changing the target/reference frame or information boundary changes task meaning; changing only evaluator/calibration state does not. A canonical-grid change can preserve scientific meaning while breaking release compatibility. **Evidence:** C1; [identity compatibility implementation](../src/fpdbench/benchmarks/base.py), [reproducibility model](reproducibility.md), A1's versioning decisions.

The historical absolute-position measurement repair illustrates this separation: eligibility, measurements and surviving membership changed while the absolute-XY target, conditional information, horizon and benchmark identity remained fixed. Origin-relative displacement instead represents a different target and reference frame and has a separate identity. The [parity matrix](../src/fpdbench/benchmarks/conditional_multi_agent_motion_prediction/displacement_reconstruction.py) preserves shared properties and unknown properties separately; it does not assert complete architecture, baseline, model-selection or calibrated-scorer parity. These are established repository semantics, with publication novelty provisional under C1.

## 2. Historical reconstruction methodology

Reconstruction proceeds from the frozen evidence registry to explicit contracts and evidence-bound records. First, identify the surviving task, data, scorer and model records and preserve their source identifiers and SHA-256 references. Second, separate original measurement/membership snapshots from later repairs and distinguish metric kernels, score transforms and calibration instances. Third, recover only supported task and evaluator behavior, preserving unavailable facts as `UNKNOWN` or a typed unavailable state. Fourth, attach each result to its population, evaluator state, validity, checkpoint/output references and scientific binding. Finally, verify public mathematical behavior and metadata consistency without treating those checks as historical prediction replay. **Evidence:** A1/A3/A5–A7; [provenance aliases](../src/fpdbench/provenance/aliases.py), [evidence inventory](../src/fpdbench/provenance/evidence_inventory.py), [historical reconstruction](../src/fpdbench/benchmarks/conditional_multi_agent_motion_prediction/displacement_reconstruction.py), [result provenance](results-and-provenance.md).

The recovered conditional-motion contracts use a historical 25 Hz source and 5 Hz model grid, fixed arrays of 25 history steps and 15 future steps, and directed scenes with 11 target players, 11 opponents and a ball. The repaired-state registry record preserves causal endpoint velocity measurements and normalization fitted on retained training histories. Those are recovered specifications and historical evidence, not newly executed preprocessing. The original and repaired population snapshots have no resolved row-level crosswalk, so their counts and outcomes remain separately scoped. **Evidence:** C2/C6, A1/A4/A7; registry `r2/conditional_team_response_absolute_position/BENCHMARK_RECONSTRUCTION.md`, `DATA_STATES.json` and `SPLIT_PROTOCOLS.json`, resolved through the accepted matrix's registry convention; [historical input contract](../src/fpdbench/benchmarks/conditional_multi_agent_motion_prediction/absolute_position_prediction.py).

Historical failures remain part of the reconstruction. The original absolute-position model did not beat simple physical baselines on its recorded original scope; later repaired-state match-held-out records passed their own viability gates. These observations concern different data/evaluation scopes. The synthetic sensor pilot selected no target family, the acceleration formulation remains invalidated without a recovered single invalidation trigger, and the whole-session study lacks enough authority for standalone execution. These statements do not establish universal impossibility, a shared failure mechanism or a football-field result for the synthetic studies. **Evidence:** C6/C7, A6's exact negative-finding rows and A7's sealed-result references; [historical transition decisions](benchmark-transitions.md).

An original displacement aggregate was invalidated after a tuple-order assignment error. The current reconstruction retains the invalidation and an aggregation-only correction from preserved fold outputs/checkpoint hashes, without retraining. The corrected record is distinct from the invalidated aggregate and from a fresh execution. Earlier registry summaries that lacked the correction remain evidence of their earlier recovery scope; the current SHA-bound correction supplies the later state. No missing fold score or calibration instance is inferred from a narrative summary. **Evidence:** A6/A7; `INVALIDATED_SECOND_CAMPAIGN_AGGREGATE`, `CORRECTED_AGGREGATION`, `CORRECTED_LOMO_AGGREGATE` in [displacement_reconstruction.py](../src/fpdbench/benchmarks/conditional_multi_agent_motion_prediction/displacement_reconstruction.py).

This methodology establishes an auditable reconstruction and public contract implementation. It does not establish a full source-to-fixture rerun, checkpoint inference, or equivalence of unresolved historical populations. Historical selection information stays unknown where it was not recovered. **Evidence:** C5–C7; [capability boundary](reproducibility.md#public-reproducibility-boundary), [historical records](../src/fpdbench/benchmarks/conditional_multi_agent_motion_prediction/displacement_reconstruction.py).

## 3. Information-boundary formalism

For a directed scene at forecast origin `t`, let `H_t` denote authorized scene history through `t`, `B_t+` the realized future ball trajectory and `O_t+` the realized future opponent trajectories over the horizon. Let `Y_t+` denote target-team future positions, withheld from learned inputs. Observation masks accompany the corresponding observable coordinates. The prospective information regimes are the following ordered input bundles; the historical conditional task corresponds to the richest bundle with its distinct fixed-array contract:

```text
I_history(t) = H_t
I_ball(t)    = (H_t, B_t+)
I_full(t)    = (H_t, B_t+, O_t+)
prediction  = f_theta(I_regime(t))
target      = T(Y_t+, observed target origin XY)
```

This notation specifies available information, not a probability model or a causal identification theorem. Realized future context is explicitly ex-post information. A conditional prediction score cannot be interpreted as an online forecasting score under history alone or as the effect of intervening on another player's trajectory. Conditional sports prediction is already present in published work, including [Yeh et al.](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/html/Yeh_Diverse_Generation_for_Multi-Agent_Sports_Games_CVPR_2019_paper.html) and [Victor et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00521-021-05888-w); C2 concerns the explicit evaluation contract. **Evidence:** A2/A8; [historical information boundary](../src/fpdbench/benchmarks/conditional_multi_agent_motion_prediction/information.py), [prospective protocol v2](prospective-skillcorner-protocol-v2.md).

`ForecastOrigin` binds finite, strictly increasing history times no later than `t`; the historical displacement input additionally requires the origin XY frame to equal the final observed history frame. Future ball/opponent context belongs in separate conditional-input fields, not the origin's past/current information. Feature-name checks reject specified target-future, target-derived and identity channels. These are executable contract checks, not proof that arbitrarily constructed upstream features contain no hidden leakage. **Evidence:** A2; [forecast_origin.py](../src/fpdbench/benchmarks/conditional_multi_agent_motion_prediction/forecast_origin.py), [displacement inputs](../src/fpdbench/benchmarks/conditional_multi_agent_motion_prediction/origin_relative_displacement_prediction.py), [contract tests](../tests/unit/test_benchmark_contracts.py).

For the prospective study, source-presence masks for authorized history/context coordinates are input features. Target truth masks, future target membership, eligibility results, phase/event/possession labels, detection attribution, quality audits, absolute match clock, file identity, row order and numeric identity encodings are not learned features. Provider IDs align records within a match and do not become cross-match identity embeddings. Membership and truth masks can determine what is scored but are not exposed as future-target information. Comparisons therefore require common windows, origin-player panels and truth masks across regimes and representations. This conditions interpretation on the eligible population; it does not demonstrate representativeness of all football situations. **Evidence:** C2/C8; [protocol v2](prospective-skillcorner-protocol-v2.md), [base prospective protocol](prospective-skillcorner-protocol.md), [population qualification contract](prospective-population-qualification-v2.md).

## 4. Targets, reference frames and evaluators

### 4.1 Historical absolute XY and origin-relative displacement

Let `p[r,h,i]` be physical target XY in metres for row `r`, future step `h` and player `i`; let `o[r,i]` be that player's observed origin XY. With componentwise scale `S = (52.5 m, 34.0 m)`, the historical normalized representations and displacement inverse are:

```text
absolute target a[r,h,i] = p[r,h,i] / S
displacement d[r,h,i]   = (p[r,h,i] - o[r,i]) / S
inverse(d, o)           = S * d + o
```

Both targets have shape `15 × 11 × 2`, but refer to different scientific outputs. An absolute-XY model may internally decode offsets and invert them before scoring; that implementation choice does not change its benchmark target. The origin must be observed, not recovered from a future label. **Evidence:** C3/A3; [absolute target](../src/fpdbench/benchmarks/conditional_multi_agent_motion_prediction/absolute_position_prediction.py), [displacement transform and inverse](../src/fpdbench/benchmarks/conditional_multi_agent_motion_prediction/origin_relative_displacement_prediction.py), [evaluation specification](evaluation.md).

Inverting prediction and truth with the same origin preserves physical residuals: `inverse(d_hat,o) − inverse(d,o) = S * (d_hat − d)`. This is an algebraic property of the implemented transform. The public synthetic reference verifies its round trip within `1e-12` metres; it supplies no football accuracy finding. Because origins vary across evaluated rows, subtracting them can change the across-row truth variance. Equal physical residuals therefore do not imply equal absolute/displacement standardized error. **Evidence:** C3; transform implementation above, [synthetic reproducibility reference](reproducibility.md#public-reproducibility-boundary), A3's round-trip and raw-evaluator tests.

### 4.2 Historical population SRE and calibrated scoring

Index the 330 scalar targets by `j`. Over the full declared evaluated population of `N` rows, define:

```text
RMSE_j = sqrt(sum_r (y_hat[r,j] - y[r,j])^2 / N)
mu_j   = sum_r y[r,j] / N
SD_j   = sqrt(sum_r (y[r,j] - mu_j)^2 / N)
SRE_j  = RMSE_j / SD_j, if SD_j > 0
         RMSE_j,        if SD_j = 0 in these historical task bindings
raw displacement score = sum_j SRE_j / 330
```

The denominator uses evaluated-population variance with `ddof=0`, not training statistics or an average of independent row scores. Nonzero-variance SRE is dimensionless; the zero-variance fallback retains normalized target units. Raw displacement scores are not clipped and lower is better. The generic metric defaults to an error on constant truth unless its caller explicitly selects fallback; the historical task bindings select RMSE fallback. **Evidence:** C3/A3; [raw metrics](../src/fpdbench/evaluation/metrics/relative_error.py), [displacement evaluator](../src/fpdbench/evaluation/displacement.py), [evaluation definitions](evaluation.md).

The absolute-position evaluator produces per-target SRE, then uses the frozen floors, ceilings and equal target weights to compute progress and a locked clamped piecewise-linear score. For target floor `f_j` and no-information ceiling `c_j`, progress is `clamp(1 − SRE_j/min(f_j,c_j), 0, 1)`. The calibration maps aggregate progress to the fixed no-information, reference and perfect anchors. These anchors define a scoring transform, not measured validation/LOMO performance. The absolute lock cannot supply the missing generated displacement calibration, reference vector, ceilings or calibrated reward. **Evidence:** A3; [bundled absolute calibration](../src/fpdbench/evaluation/absolute_xy_calibration_lock.json), [scorers](../src/fpdbench/evaluation/scorers.py), [evaluation definitions](evaluation.md).

### 4.3 Physical diagnostics and prospective masks

For physical residual vector `e[r,h,i]` on a complete historical panel, ADE is the mean Euclidean norm over rows, steps and players; FDE is the mean norm at the final step; XY-RMSE is the square root of the mean squared residual over both scalar coordinates. Historical normalized residuals are first scaled by `S`. These physical diagnostics have distinct evaluator bindings from historical raw SRE and calibrated reward. **Evidence:** A3/A5; [trajectory kernels](../src/fpdbench/evaluation/metrics/trajectory.py), [physical diagnostic lock](../benchmarks/conditional_multi_agent_motion_prediction/public_validation_physical_diagnostic_scientific_lock.json), [evaluation definitions](evaluation.md).

Prospective targets use provider pitch-centered XY or `p − o` directly in metres, without historical pitch normalization, rotation or attack-direction normalization. Panels have variable cardinality fixed from the finite-XY origin players. A target absent or missing either coordinate at a future step has a null XY truth pair; no replacement player or imputed truth is introduced. Every prediction must remain finite for every origin target and future step, even at masked truth positions. **Evidence:** C3/C8; [prospective target and missingness contract](prospective-skillcorner-protocol.md), [protocol v2](prospective-skillcorner-protocol-v2.md), [prospective metric tests](../tests/unit/test_prospective_trajectory_metrics.py).

Within a scoreable directed scene, let `V` be the set of valid player/time truth pairs and `V_H` the valid final-step players. ADE is `sum_V ||e|| / |V|`, FDE is `sum_V_H ||e_H|| / |V_H|`, and XY-RMSE is `sqrt(sum_V (e_x² + e_y²)/(2|V|))`. A scene with no valid final target is excluded from all three metrics with `NO_FINAL_TARGET`; a window with no scoreable scene has no score. This shared scoreability rule prevents the metrics silently evaluating different scene populations. Prospective scoring produces physical metrics, not historical SRE or calibrated reward. **Evidence:** A8; [prospective evaluation contract](prospective-skillcorner-protocol.md#metrics-and-uncertainty), [mask-aware kernels](../src/fpdbench/evaluation/metrics/trajectory.py), [population-bound evaluator](../src/fpdbench/benchmarks/conditional_multi_agent_motion_prediction/prospective_evaluator.py).

## 5. Match-level generalization and statistical uncertainty

The physical match is the experimental and cross-match aggregation unit. All overlapping windows and both directed scenes from a physical window remain in the same match role/fold. Historical canonical roles include five public training matches, public validation match J03WN1, and metadata-only private qualification match J03WMX; there is no public test population. Historical canonical LOMO holds out one training match and fits on the other four. Validation and private qualification matches are excluded from every such fold. Shared role assignments do not imply identical fixture membership across benchmark targets, and canonical protocols do not retroactively rebind recovered historical results. **Evidence:** C4/A4; [canonical protocols](../src/fpdbench/protocols.py), [role table and historical membership semantics](reproducibility.md#canonical-conditional-motion-split-protocols).

For match `m` and seed `s`, let `q_ms` be the metric on that match's full declared population, using the bound evaluator. With `S_n` seeds and `M` held-out matches, the implemented match-first descriptive summary is:

```text
q_m     = sum_s q_ms / S_n
q_bar   = sum_m q_m / M
SD_match = sqrt(sum_m (q_m - q_bar)^2 / (M - 1)), for M > 1
seed_mean_s = sum_m q_ms / M
```

Seed sensitivity is summarized separately from the `seed_mean_s` values. For a balanced historical three-seed/five-match table, the grand mean can equal the mean of all 15 cells while the generalization unit count remains five. Population SRE must be computed across each declared match population before this cross-match aggregation; averaging arbitrary row-level SRE would change the metric. **Evidence:** C4; `summarize_lomo_scores` and `summarize_match_scores` in [protocols.py](../src/fpdbench/protocols.py), [aggregation tests](../tests/unit/test_protocols.py), [evaluation definitions](evaluation.md).

A single validation match gives a point estimate and no between-match SD, SE or CI. Historical LOMO supplies descriptive held-out-match SD, but its fitted models share training matches; the current method contract declares no dependence-aware inferential SE or CI. The broader utility's explicit independent-match SE option is not invoked as a justification for LOMO inference. This treatment is consistent with structured validation concerns in [Roberts et al.](https://doi.org/10.1111/ecog.02881) and CV-variance limitations in [Bengio and Grandvalet](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v5/grandvalet04a.html); it does not rule out all future dependence-aware inference. **Evidence:** C4/A4; [uncertainty implementation](../src/fpdbench/experiments/uncertainty.py), [result uncertainty contract](results-and-provenance.md#uncertainty), [protocols.py](../src/fpdbench/protocols.py).

For the prospective protocol, each metric is aggregated over valid labels within a directed scene, equally over scoreable scenes within a window, equally over scoreable windows within a match, and equally over the four evaluation matches. Seed results are averaged within each match before contrasts. A match with no scoreable window remains in the declared population and blocks the complete four-match summary; it is not dropped. This estimand differs from pooling all frames or weighting matches by their number of windows. No p-values, confidence intervals, significance or multiplicity-adjusted claims are authorized for the frozen descriptive contrasts. **Evidence:** C4/C8; [prospective aggregation and uncertainty](prospective-skillcorner-protocol.md#metrics-and-uncertainty), [frozen plan](prospective-experiment-plan-v2.md).

These protocols evaluate held-out matches in their declared cohorts. They establish neither independence of all football matches nor transfer across leagues, seasons, teams or providers. More windows or repeated seeds do not increase the number of held-out physical matches. Broader domain generalization remains an empirical dependency under C4/C8; [WILDS](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/koh21a.html) supplies prior methodology rather than evidence that FPD has tested such breadth.

## 6. Scientific locks, provenance and capability-bounded reproducibility

A scientific lock hashes result-bearing benchmark definition, data release/manifest and state identity, split, evaluator, schema, fixture membership and scientific-provenance snapshot fields. Its canonical serialization and digest validation are implemented in the shared lock module. Legal status, hosting, artifact locations, publication state and timestamps belong to release/governance metadata outside this scientific identity. A changed evaluator or membership therefore requires the corresponding scientific binding even when the benchmark ID remains the same. **Evidence:** C1/C5, A5; [scientific_lock.py](../src/fpdbench/provenance/scientific_lock.py), [reproducibility model](reproducibility.md), [scientific-lock tests](../tests/unit/test_data_and_lock.py).

An experiment adds system identity, run configuration digest, model-selection declaration, environment and artifact references to a verified `ScientificStateBinding`. A result adds typed metrics/units, population, validity, uncertainty, output digests and evidence. Result outputs do not alter the lock they consume. Historical recovered records use `HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE_BOUND`; missing environments or training configuration are not fabricated to imitate a newly executed `EXECUTION_MANIFEST_BOUND` record. Artifact bytes identify content; a URI describes location. **Evidence:** C5/A5; [manifest implementation](../src/fpdbench/experiments/manifests.py), [result types](../src/fpdbench/experiments/results.py), [provenance definitions](results-and-provenance.md).

Reproducibility is reported as an operation-specific capability, rather than one blanket property:

| Capability | Scope in the current public checkout |
| --- | --- |
| `PUBLIC_EXECUTABLE` | Target transforms/inversion, information-boundary checks, evaluators, absolute calibration, protocol/lock construction and historical result-manifest hashing. |
| `PUBLIC_VERIFIABLE` | Historical fixture/population metadata, checkpoint identifiers and result/output references. These do not provide underlying prediction or checkpoint bytes. |
| `LOCAL_EVIDENCE_VERIFIABLE` | Canonical registry evidence resolution under the documented registry boundary. |
| `UNAVAILABLE_PUBLIC_ARTIFACT` | Historical source-to-full-fixture regeneration, learned checkpoint loading and deterministic learned inference. |
| `FORBIDDEN_PRIVATE_TRUTH` | J03WMX private qualification truth evaluation. No private result is generated. |

The capability table is backed by `CAPABILITY_MATRIX` in [reproducibility.py](../src/fpdbench/reproducibility.py) and the [frozen snapshot](../benchmarks/reproducibility_snapshot.json), not inferred from passing tests alone. The snapshot records deterministic public state, and the evidence inventory checks public SHA-reference structure. Optional strict evidence resolution is a separate operation; shape validation alone does not authenticate all external bytes. Hash agreement proves content identity under the specified serialization, not scientific correctness or permission to release data. **Evidence:** C5/A5; [public boundary](reproducibility.md#public-reproducibility-boundary), [evidence resolution](../src/fpdbench/provenance/evidence_resolution.py), [rights policy](data-rights.md).

The established methodological result is an executable and inspectable boundary between these capabilities. Public synthetic transformation, calibration and rejection tests support mathematical/software behavior only. They cannot substitute for historical football inference, real-source qualification, or an empirical demonstration. The disclosure complements, rather than replaces, reproducibility reporting and dataset documentation in [Pineau et al.](https://jmlr.org/papers/v22/20-303.html) and [Gebru et al.](https://doi.org/10.1145/3458723). **Evidence:** C5/C8; [public reproducibility reference](reproducibility.md), [synthetic prospective verifier scope](prospective-population-verifier-v2.md).

## 7. Prospective FPD Bench v1 methodology

### 7.1 Source, authority versions and population

“FPD Bench v1” names the prospective public-source research program in the parent issue, not permission to mix authority versions. The frozen base protocol is `skillcorner.prospective_conditional_motion` v1.1.0. Its additive nullable-ball successor protocol and execution plan are v2.0.0, with separate measurement, eligibility and schema authorities. The successor preserves the planned match roles, task geometry, regimes, contrasts and experiment choices; it does not modify the frozen base or historical locks. The prospective benchmark IDs are `skillcorner.prospective.conditional_motion.absolute_xy` and `skillcorner.prospective.conditional_motion.origin_relative_displacement`, advancing to version 2.0.0 under the successor. They are new prospective identities, not aliases or repaired memberships of the historical benchmarks. **Evidence:** C8/A8; [base protocol](prospective-skillcorner-protocol.md), [successor protocol](prospective-skillcorner-protocol-v2.md), [protocol v2 machine authority](../src/fpdbench/benchmarks/conditional_multi_agent_motion_prediction/prospective_skillcorner_protocol_v2.json).

The source authority pins SkillCorner Open Data at commit `4340d274572876239c154c90bc507a9b3250a656` and an explicit 20-match source population. Measurement retains globally even 10 Hz source-frame numbers to define a 5 Hz grid, preserving provider pitch-centered coordinates in metres and gaps/missingness without interpolation, smoothing or imputation. Within each period, a prospective five-second history comprises 25 intervals and 26 samples including the origin; 15 future intervals contribute 15 more samples, for 41 support samples. Origin-player panels are variable cardinality and fixed through the horizon; later entrants do not become replacement targets. The historical fixed 25-sample/11-player arrays must not be substituted. **Evidence:** A8; [source authority](skillcorner-source-authority.md), [measurement contract](skillcorner-data-state.md), [measurement v2](skillcorner-data-state-v2.md), [prospective temporal/target contract](prospective-skillcorner-protocol.md).

The successor separates ball position availability from detection attribution. Source-false finite XY remains a position. A null ball object remains absent; true/false/null flags are not coerced. Candidate ball support requires finite origin XY and at least 39 both-finite positions among the 41 expected slots, alongside all other frozen eligibility rules. A null detection flag with finite XY triggers a population-wide stop before membership/model masks; filtering affected windows is not a permitted remedy. These are policy definitions with synthetic verification, not measured source-quality frequencies or qualified window counts. **Evidence:** C8/A8; [v2 measurement](skillcorner-data-state-v2.md), [v2 eligibility](skillcorner-window-eligibility-v2.md), [eligibility machine authority](../src/fpdbench/eligibility/skillcorner_window_v2.json).

The frozen split assigns 12 training, four validation and four final evaluation matches, with all windows/scenes from a match kept together. Twelve descriptive LOMO folds hold out one training-role match and fit on the other 11; validation/evaluation matches never enter fold training. The exact match IDs and role algorithm remain those of the protocol, independent of eventual model outcomes. These are design counts, not evidence that every match has qualified scoreable windows. **Evidence:** C4/C8; [protocol split contract](prospective-skillcorner-protocol.md#match-assignment-and-lomo), [protocol v2 JSON](../src/fpdbench/benchmarks/conditional_multi_agent_motion_prediction/prospective_skillcorner_protocol_v2.json).

### 7.2 Frozen model matrix and selection

Three information regimes cross two representations and three model classes for 18 planned cells. The deterministic controls are origin persistence and constant velocity estimated from the preceding one-second history. The single primary learned class is `per_target_context_deepsets_mlp_v1`; its architecture, masks, optimizer, loss and sampling rules remain those of the frozen execution plan. The controls ignore future context, so their repeated regime cells are controls rather than separate evidence of a context effect. No architecture search or new baseline is added by this manuscript. **Evidence:** C8; [model definitions in the base plan](prospective-experiment-plan.md#frozen-matrix), [successor plan](prospective-experiment-plan-v2.md), [plan v2 JSON](../src/fpdbench/benchmarks/conditional_multi_agent_motion_prediction/empirical_experiment_plan_v2.json).

The plan fixes seeds 1729, 2718 and 31415 across comparable learned cells. Six learned cells across three seeds give 18 final fits; twelve LOMO folds give 216 further fits, for 234 planned learned fits. Checkpoints minimize the equal mean of validation-match ADE, with earliest-step tie breaking; validation checks occur every 100 optimizer steps, with ten checks without strict improvement or 5,000 steps as stopping limits, also subject to frozen compute ceilings. Evaluation and LOMO outcomes cannot select or tune models. The plan bounds the campaign at 312 CPU-core-hours and 336 wall-clock hours and specifies limited identical-configuration infrastructure retry; outcomes cannot justify extra cells, seeds or budget. These are prospective constraints, not executed fits or measured compute use. **Evidence:** A8; [selection and compute contract](prospective-experiment-plan.md), [successor plan](prospective-experiment-plan-v2.md), [machine selection/failure rules](../src/fpdbench/benchmarks/conditional_multi_agent_motion_prediction/empirical_experiment_plan_v2.json).

### 7.3 Evaluation, contrasts and qualification gate

Primary evaluation is physical ADE in metres; FDE and XY-RMSE are secondary diagnostics. For each representation, the two primary information contrasts are history-only ADE minus future-ball ADE and future-ball ADE minus full-context ADE, using the primary learned class. Average seeds within each evaluation match, report every matched difference and then the equal-match mean only when all four pairs are scoreable. Positive differences would mean lower ADE with added realized context. Their signs and magnitudes remain unknown; all other model/metric comparisons are exploratory or descriptive. **Evidence:** C8; [four frozen contrasts](prospective-experiment-plan.md), [prospective analysis contract](prospective-skillcorner-protocol.md), [plan v2 JSON](../src/fpdbench/benchmarks/conditional_multi_agent_motion_prediction/empirical_experiment_plan_v2.json).

Before any empirical baseline, training, validation selection, LOMO or final scoring, the exact 20-match population must be independently qualified.
