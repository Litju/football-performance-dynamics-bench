## Related-work synthesis

### Football and team-sports trajectories

Yeh et al. already model joint sports trajectories and conditional futures; their evaluations include generative sampling criteria [L1]. Victor et al. are especially close: they study soccer, linear extrapolation, and predicting some players given other players' full trajectories [L2]. Capellera et al. unify forecasting and trajectory completion with uncertainty estimates on several sports datasets, including Soccer-U [L3]. These precedents rule out claiming conditional football prediction itself as FPD Bench's novelty. Their published scores are not a leaderboard for FPD: source populations, available future observations, sampling rules, and metrics must first match.

Paper A's proposed distinction is an inspectable evaluation contract around those choices, including explicitly unavailable evidence. It does not contribute a new trajectory architecture. Its deterministic physical diagnostics also should not be equated with a best-of-N generative evaluation, calibrated predictive uncertainty, or a team-plausibility score.

### Information boundary and leakage

Kapoor and Narayanan document leakage as a scientific validity and reproducibility problem and propose more explicit reporting [L4]. FPD specializes that concern to history, realized future ball/opponent context, withheld target futures, identity features, and preprocessing. Future context is authorized in a conditional task; calling that task online forecasting would misstate the available information. The name-based validator rejects specified feature names, while `ForecastOrigin` constrains observation times and origin coordinates. Neither proves arbitrary upstream features are free of hidden target information. The three prospective regimes provide a controlled design for studying available information; no context-benefit result exists yet.

### Splits and cross-match generalization

Blocked validation for dependent data predates FPD [L5]; WILDS likewise makes distribution shifts part of benchmark design [L7]. FPD's physical match unit keeps overlapping windows and both directed scenes together and makes match/seed aggregation explicit. This is a football-specific application, not a new cross-validation estimator. Bengio and Grandvalet establish a general limitation on unbiased variance estimation from K-fold errors [L6]. FPD consequently reports descriptive held-out-match dispersion separately from seed sensitivity and withholds inferential intervals under its current method contract. That policy does not imply all possible dependence-aware inference is impossible.

### Reference frames and evaluation objects

Sports trajectory and completion methods provide the nearest application context [L1–L3]. FPD distinguishes the target's reference frame from a decoder's internal representation and from the scorer. A decoder producing offsets that are inverted before absolute-XY scoring does not turn the absolute-position task into a displacement benchmark. FPD's separate benchmark identities encode its scientific questions; no new coordinate transform is claimed. The reference-frame contribution is the explicit contract and comparability analysis in C3, not a literature-wide priority claim.

### Reproducibility, audits, and negative findings

Pineau et al. motivate reproducibility through code, challenges, and reporting [L8]; datasheets document dataset construction and intended use [L9]. FPD adds explicit scientific-state bindings and capability labels so executable formulas, inspectable metadata, inaccessible historical artifacts, and forbidden truth are not conflated. This is a proposed benchmark-methodology contribution; hashes alone cannot establish empirical validity.

Recht et al. demonstrate that benchmark conclusions can change on newly collected evaluation data [L10]. Together with the baseline caution in [L2] and leakage audits in [L4], this motivates retaining failed, corrected, and unrecoverable outcomes. It does not demonstrate that FPD's historical failures share those papers' causes. Paper A's audit concerns this project's evidence and lineage, not a measured failure rate across football research.

## Claim wording for the methods draft

The defensible contribution paragraph is: “We present an evidence-bound framework for specifying and evaluating multi-agent football motion tasks. It distinguishes allowed information, target/reference frame, measurement state, split unit and evaluator identity; implements public transformation and scoring checks; and records the boundary between executable reproduction, verifiable historical metadata and unavailable artifacts. A reconstructed case history preserves negative findings and corrected aggregates. A separately frozen prospective study specifies matched information-regime comparisons; its empirical demonstration remains pending population qualification.”

Do not replace “specifies” with “demonstrates improved prediction,” or “evidence-bound” with “fully reproducible.” Do not describe conditional future context as causal identification, convert the absolute calibration anchor into a measured score, compare absolute/displacement raw SRE as accuracy on one scale, or turn synthetic reconciliation/runtime measurements into football observations.

## Remaining scientific gaps

1. **Prospective demonstration:** independently qualify the exact real 20-match population and common windows/panels/masks before any baseline, selection, LOMO or scoring. Then execute only the frozen study under its separate authorization. No empirical context, representation or robustness finding is available for Paper A today.

Recover displacement calibration, exact missing parity and historical selection details only from evidence. 3. **Reproduction:** public historical source-to-fixture regeneration and learned checkpoint inference remain unavailable. This review verifies the public metadata/capability boundary; it does not rerun protected predictions or fully resolve external source bytes.
4. **Generalization and uncertainty:** five historical LOMO match units and one public-validation match support limited descriptive statements. Prospective four-match evaluation is also descriptive by contract. Broader league/season/provider claims and inferential uncertainty need separately designed evidence, not row-level resampling or more seeds presented as more matches.
5. **Contribution positioning:** C1–C6 are supported as implemented methodology/case evidence, while their publication novelty remains provisional. A later manuscript review must compare concrete benchmark tooling and fully harmonized external protocols before claiming priority or superiority.
